deslop blocks a comma in a short display heading. The rule: if a headline needs a comma it is usually two ideas and too long, like "One login, every tool". It is scoped to headings of six words or fewer, so long list-style headings and ordinary doc headings are spared, and it excludes the legitimate cases the hook already allowed: a date ("September 4, 2026"), an "X, or Y?" decision framing, an "X, or ..." explanatory heading ("Kindred invites, or how circles grow"), and a schedule gloss ("Migration, in three weeks"). Three of the skills' own doc headings were reworded to comply.
