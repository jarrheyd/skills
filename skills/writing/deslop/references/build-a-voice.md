# Build a voice from someone's own writing

Removing tells leaves prose with no voice. When there is no `house-rules.md` in `$DESLOP_DATA` yet, build the voice from the person's own writing before any rewrite or draft. Never borrow another author's voice, this skill's examples included.

## 1. Collect real samples

Only text the person typed themselves. Skip anything AI drafted, even if they sent it.

- Chat: their last 20-30 messages in the target channel (Slack, Discord, Teams, WhatsApp). Read them through whatever tool is connected, or ask them to paste 10.
- Email: 10 sent replies, ideally to the same reader or the same kind of reader.
- Documents: 2-3 docs, proposals or briefs they wrote themselves, best from before they used AI. Ask which ones; do not guess.
- Corrections: any line they rejected and what they wrote instead. One corrected draft teaches more than ten samples.

Same channel beats same person beats same register. If none is reachable, ask for a paste and wait. Do not draft from a guess.

## 2. Measure what they do

Write down what the samples show, with counts where you can:

- Length: words per message or paragraph, and how often they split one thought across sends.
- Casing and punctuation: capitals, periods at the end, question marks, dashes, emoji, "lol" or "haha".
- Openers and closers: how a message starts, whether there is a greeting or sign-off at all.
- How they ask, how they say yes, how they say no, how they push back.
- Words and particles they use often, words they never use, languages they mix.
- Structure: lists or none, headings or none, how a document opens.

## 3. Save it

Write the result to `$DESLOP_DATA/house-rules.md` under a `## Voice` heading, with 5-10 of their real lines quoted as the reference. Add their own taste rules there too (spelling standard, banned words, fonts). Its presence also turns on the house hook checks.

## 4. Draft against the samples

Shape every draft against the quoted lines, not against the description. Then test it: drop the draft into the channel's scroll in your head. If it is longer, tidier or more polite than the messages around it, rewrite it.
